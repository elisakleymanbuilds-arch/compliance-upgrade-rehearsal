Overview & Institutional Context

Traditional compliance records whether people complete physical drills. This prototype adds a narrow decision test: can a person recognize when a validated safety cue means the rehearsed sequence should be interrupted?

What this prototype measures

  • Recognition of a validated safety cue
  • Reassessment of a rehearsed sequence
  • Transfer of that judgment to a new scenario

The physical earthquake drill is still required. This simulation complements that process rather than replacing it.
