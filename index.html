Overview & Institutional Context

Measure Reassessment Judgment & Transfer

Traditional compliance records attendance at physical drills, but cannot safely test whether staff persist on an evacuation route when a valid safety hazard contradicts the rehearsed plan.

This 3-minute digital check tests one specific judgment: recognizing when a validated hazard requires pausing and redirecting, and tests if that judgment transfers to a different situation.
